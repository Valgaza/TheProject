# Lakeview Skin Clinic: Clinician Handbook

A fictional document written for the Nexus demonstration; Lakeview Skin Clinic
and everyone named here are invented.

## Clinic hours

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00. It is closed on Sundays.
New patient appointments are 20 minutes long; review appointments are 10
minutes long.

## People

- Dr. Farhan Iyer is the consultant dermatologist. He sees referred patients on
  Tuesdays and Fridays.
- Sister Grace Thomas runs the treatment room and the dressing clinic.
- Rahul Desai is the clinic pharmacist.

## Referrals

Refer a patient to Dr. Farhan Iyer through the clinic referral form. Mark the
form urgent for spreading cellulitis or any suspected skin cancer; urgent
referrals are seen within 48 hours.

## Laboratory

Samples go to the laboratory at 15:00 each day.

- Liver function test (LFT) results come back within 24 hours.
- KOH microscopy results come back the same day.
- Fungal culture results take 3 weeks.

## Pharmacy stock

The clinic pharmacy stocks terbinafine 1% cream, clotrimazole 1% cream, oral
terbinafine 250 mg tablets, oral itraconazole 100 mg capsules and mometasone
0.1% cream. Oral fluconazole is not stocked and must be prescribed for an
outside pharmacy.
